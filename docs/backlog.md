| 16 | M4 | **The two code paths do not fill an order larger than one book state identically.** A backtest matches a queued command inside `process_bar`, where a bar is replayed as its synthesised tick sequence, so a market order that exhausts one price level completes at the next one. `SandboxExecutionClient` builds its exchange with `use_message_queue=False`, so the order is matched in the call that submits it, against one book state, and the remainder is never filled and never reported — the node emits one `OrderFilled` per order and no cancellation, where the engine emits 325 fills for the same 191 orders. Reproduced on a synthetic month whose price collapses ~97% while the strategy sizes from a fixed notional: order sizes reach tens of thousands of shares, and `replay parity` reports `intent 47: qty is 739.0 on the node path and 976.0 on the engine path` — the engine filled 739 at 10.24 and 237 at 10.25, the node filled 739 and dropped 237. | The fix is deferred, but the failure now explains itself. A divergence whose quantity is lower on the node path carries the cause and the check that confirms it — compare the order's size with the volume of the bar it was sent on — in `kanso replay parity` and in the gate's evidence, so an operator who hits this does not have to rediscover it. Only that shape is named; a side, an instrument, an instant, or a quantity higher on the node is left unexplained rather than given a cause that may not be its own. The `sandbox` docstring states the difference and the depth below which the paths agree, rather than claiming they always do. Below that depth they agree to the nanosecond — 850 intents over the demo's certification window and 504 over its forward window, zero divergence. Three things were tried and did not fix it: a second `exchange.process` after the strategy acts, setting `use_message_queue` on the exchange after construction, and the config — which exposes neither that flag nor `fee_model` nor `fill_model`. Neither path emits an `OrderCanceled`, `OrderRejected` or `OrderDenied`, so the shortfall is a fill that never happens rather than an order the venue refused. | M7, with the broker execution client and the sandbox-versus-broker parity test. The fix is for kanso to build its own `SimulatedExchange` and `BacktestExecClient` pair — the engine's own components, ~40 lines — instead of using `SandboxExecutionClient`, so it can pass `use_message_queue=True` and `fee_model=None` and stop depending on the zero-fee instrument invariant to keep the sandbox cost-neutral. Until then, a hypothesis whose orders exceed a bar's depth at one price will fail its required `parity_replay` gate, and that failure is honest. |
