worked around rather than stopped for. Nothing here blocks a later milestone; all of it is
worth an operator's eye.

A closed entry stays, with the claim that closed struck through and what closed it beside
it, because what was found is worth as much to a reader as what remains — and an entry
half closed says which half, so the half still open is not read as done. `CHANGELOG.md`
records the same closures as user-visible changes.
